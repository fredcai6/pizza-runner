Short read. Grotesque survives 124 px when it is carried by big flat shapes: the tongue, the fanged grin, the bulging eyeballs and the droopy noodle limbs. Qwen with a Real Monsters style reference (D, E) is the best of these and is bolder than the current art without losing Vinny. Fine detail (veins, drool, lumpy cheese, halftone dots) turns into noise at 1x: veins become two red pixels, which is fine, but lumps do nothing. Neither model puts the hard shadow on the figure: both paint it as a cast shadow on the floor or backdrop, or, when pushed (Qwen C), as a black block that eats the face. The code-added shadow (N1 to N3) gives the actual 'solid violet-black block' look in a repeatable way.
