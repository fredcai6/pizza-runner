## 1. Voice sheet

**The Old Timer.** He talks in short cop-show declaratives and calls everyone "kid". Everything is intel and nothing is a coincidence. He wants one more case before the oven goes cold, and he needs the world to be a conspiracy because the alternative is that he's just stale. His tic is that any evidence for his theory counts, and any evidence against it counts double. ("Nothing has ever been proven otherwise.")

**Vinny Two-Fold (NY).** He drops his g's and talks in stoop-front bravado. He claims a fear is "a Tuesday where I'm from" and then shows he's scared in the next breath. He wants respect and to be first. His tic is "I fold for nobody", and every brave line sets up a smaller one.

**Big Stan (Chicago).** He's deadpan, with few words, and he's sore about the casserole thing. He wants to be left alone and to be taken seriously as pizza. His tic is a flat, quiet line that ends the exchange ("Somebody will.").

**La Contessa (Naples).** She's imperious and certified, with Italian sprinkled in. Every complaint is really a brag about the fire and the 90 seconds. She wants America to remember where all of this started. Her tic is counting seconds and measuring everyone against "in Napoli".

**Phil A. Buster (Jumbo, DC).** He talks like the Senate floor: "Mr. Chairman", "let the record show", "I yield". He wants the floor and has never once been given it. His tic is starting a speech and getting cut off, and he is the one who actually reads the fine print.

## 7. Lines I'm proudest of

1. **"You go in under cover. Lid down. Nobody looks twice at a lid."** It fixes the "deep undercover" premise with a pun that works, and it sets up the DEEP UNDERCOVER tape ("lid down, no contact") without anyone saying what happens to the slices.
2. **"Nobody spells it right unless it's theirs."** It's the Long Beach bridge. It is entirely his interpretation, it falls out of the zanier-spellings gag, and it makes Katelyn's real name the thing that gives her away.
3. **"That's what she'd want you to think."** This is Vinny's skeptic aside answered with the Old Timer's unfalsifiable logic. It lets the player see the hole without any scene debunking him.
4. **"Phil A. Buster. Jumbo slice, Washington. Three names. Only a senator needs all three."** The name joke follows from his character.
5. **"Printed right on the ticket. That's how good they are."** The Order is now the word at the top of every pizza order, and the Old Timer treats "they aren't even hiding it" as proof of how clever they are.
