# Pizza Runner script draft: The Springfield room

Speaker keys match content.js: `old`, `ny`, `deep`, `jumbo`, `naples`, `alewife`, `customer`.

**The spine.** Everything the Old Timer says follows from one rule: **"undercover" literally means under a cover.** The recruits go out under a lid, with tape on, in a box. "Deep undercover" means the bottom of the stack, and nobody has contact with it. That gives him a built-in explanation for every slice that goes quiet after delivery: no contact means it's working. The player gets the eating joke from his reports and never from a line that says it.

**Departures from Tommy's notes**
- I moved Contessa's "Mamma mia" to right after "Slices are vanishing all over the world." That is the line it reacts to.
- "Pipe down" now interrupts her mid-complaint, and he promises to get to the strings.
- "Deep undercover" survives with the lid-and-stack premise described above.
- The Order is still his named theory. He now says he got it from tapping the dispatch line, which matches the "hacked dispatch" idea in the handoff.
- I dropped the number "eight slices" from the families speech. City pies are six-slice and the finale is eight, so a number would contradict one of them.
- I cut "Sit, eat" and "Mangia" from the Naples customers. They say outright that the pizza gets eaten.
- The Central Park customer is now "Pigeon Lady". Nothing in her lines needs a bench.
- The SPEAKERS label for `jumbo` becomes "Phil A. Buster". Card lines change to match.

## 1. Voice sheet

**The Old Timer.** Clipped drill sergeant with a noir detective's filing habit. He states insane things as settled paperwork ("Intel goes on the board"). He wants to be proven right before the oven goes cold. Tic: he ends scenes on "Next." or "Think about it." Real warmth leaks out only in short flashes: "Kid, I know the feeling."

**Vinny Two-Fold.** Pure New York bravado who announces he is not scared immediately before being scared. He says "boss" every other line and drops his g's ("sayin'", "nothin'"). He wants respect and has never been indoors. Tic: "I fold for nobody", plus the "ain't sayin' I'm rattled" non-denial.

**Big Stan.** Laconic, aggrieved, literal. He speaks in short flat sentences and expects to be insulted. He wants to be taken seriously as a pizza. Tic: "...Fine." and the casserole prophecy. He never raises his voice, because that would take effort.

**La Contessa.** Theatrical, certified, and insulted by anything not Neapolitan. She quotes durations and credentials ("ninety seconds", "certified by a government") and calls Americans "America". She wants the world to remember where pizza came from, and she is quietly homesick. Tic: Italian exclamations and a dramatic "fredda" when cold.

**Phil A. Buster.** A congressional filibuster in slice form: "Mr. Chairman", "let the record show", "a point of order". He yields his time to himself. Every speech is a preamble that gets cut off. He wants to be heard in full, once. Tic: starting a four-hour statement that someone interrupts at the first comma.

## 3. The five briefing scenes

### Scene 1: Opening -> New York

**LOGIC:** A last slice with a theory needs agents. He can't go himself, and the strings he can see run through every city. So he starts in New York, where every pie passes through and nobody looks twice at a loud one. Vinny's bravado gives him the volunteer.

```
comp: wide
OLD: New recruits. Look at you. Warm, plain, and full of hope. [T]
OLD: Welcome to Pizza HQ. [T]
OLD: Slices are vanishing all over the world. [T]

comp: naples
NAPLES: Mamma mia! The whole world? And nobody tells Napoli?
OLD: Pipe down, Contessa. I'm getting to the string.

comp: board
OLD: Whole pies, gone by morning. Some nights all that turns up is the crust. It never talks.
OLD: Someone is pulling the strings. [T] I have a name for them. **The Order.**
OLD: I came up with it myself. Nobody else says it. That's how deep it goes.
OLD: I tapped the dispatch line. Every ticket is a thread. We pull back.
OLD: You go **undercover**. Under a cover. Lid down, tape on. Nobody asks a box what's inside.
OLD: And if the tape goes on twice, that's **deep undercover**. Bottom of the stack. No contact. Ever.
OLD: Now. You will go out there, get in close, and find out who. [T]

comp: naples   (card: La Contessa / Margherita of Naples)
OLD: La Contessa. Fastest thing in the oven. Fastest to cool, too. Certified a pizza by a government.
NAPLES: Ninety seconds in the fire. You would not last **nine**.

comp: deep   (card: Big Stan / Deep Dish of Chicago)
OLD: Big Stan. Two inches of sauce, four inches of grudge. Slow to start. Slower to stop.
DEEP: It's not a casserole.
OLD: Nobody said it was.
DEEP: Somebody will.

comp: jumbo   (card: Phil A. Buster / Jumbo Slice of Washington)
OLD: Phil A. Buster. Largest slice in the free world. Structural integrity, none. Three names.
JUMBO: A point of personal privilege. I wish to yield my time to myself, for brief remarks on...
OLD: You may not.

comp: ny   (card: Vinny Two-Fold / Slice of New York)
OLD: And last, Vinny Two-Fold. Off the streets. Nobody would take him indoors.
NY: Mysterious disappearances? Boss, that's a **Tuesday** where I'm from. I ain't scared a nothin'.
NY: I fold for nobody.
OLD: I like a brave slice. New York first. Nobody looks twice at a loud one.
NY: Wait, what?
OLD: Lid's open. Get in the box.
```

### Scene 2: New York returns -> Chicago

**LOGIC:** Vinny comes back rattled. The Old Timer turns that into "intel", and the board is where fear gets filed. Chicago is next on the dispatch he's tapped. He needs something that can dig and can't be pushed, and Stan is the deepest thing in the room.

```
comp: ny
NY: Boss. Boss. It's hell out there. [T]
NY: And I ain't sayin' I'm rattled. I'm sayin' some a me didn't come back. [T]
OLD: These jobs aren't for the **limp crusted**. [T]
OLD: What you saw out there is called intel. Intel goes on the board.

comp: board
OLD: Every scrap we ever pulled is up here. A lot of good slices paid for this cork.
OLD: Anything out there is an agent of The Order until proven otherwise. Nothing has been proven otherwise.
NY: Has anybody tried? Provin' it?
OLD: That's how they get you.

comp: boardTeen
OLD: **Teenagers.** Don't let them take your picture. Take one, and more of them are waiting next time.
OLD: Why would a teenager need that many pictures? Think about it.

comp: boardTurtle
OLD: **Turtles.** Slow. But I've seen them take a bite out of many a tough slice.
OLD: Slow is what they want you to think. It's also what they are.

comp: boardTease

comp: deep
OLD: Big Stan. Chicago is next on dispatch. I need someone to dig. You're the deepest thing here.
DEEP: I'm a dish. I don't dig.
OLD: Dig anyway.
DEEP: ...Fine.
```

### Scene 3: Deep Dish returns -> Washington, DC

**LOGIC:** Stan's chase gives the Old Timer a rally speech about the pizza families he protects. The mutant is proof the stakes are real. The names on the tickets are a pattern to him, and a pattern means the top. The top of a city is a capital, and the best man in the capital's own language is Phil.

```
comp: deep
DEEP: You weren't lying, boss. Something came up out of the storm drain. Some kind of mutant.
DEEP: It chased me for three blocks. I do not do three blocks.

comp: oldtimer
OLD (thump): **THAT** is why we do this!
OLD: Why we work long and hard to keep the ovens hot! So that tonight, a family sleeps warm!
OLD: A whole pie, shoulder to shoulder in the box. Lid down. Cozy. They will never know your name.
OLD: They sleep warm because you stand out there in the cold!

comp: board
OLD: And these customers. K8lyn. Cat Lynn. Kæthlynn. Tell me that's a coincidence.
NY: I mean, it could be a popular name, boss.
OLD: Popular. Like a password is popular.
OLD: This goes **all the way to the top**.

comp: jumbo
OLD: And the top is Washington. Phil. Shake down DC.
JUMBO: I accept with humility, and shall begin with a statement of no more than four hours.
OLD: Go.
```

### Scene 4: Phil returns -> Naples

**LOGIC:** Phil comes back diminished and reports one detail: every box in DC says "Original Italian". The Old Timer follows his rule that when everything repeats, it begins somewhere. Pizza began in Naples. Contessa is the only one who can go home, and she half-agrees with him. Keep it quick.

```
comp: jumbo
JUMBO: Mr. Chairman, I rise today a mere fraction of my former self.
JUMBO: I entered that city whole. The record shows nothing. It is classified.
JUMBO: But let it be noted that every box says the same. In every district. **Original Italian.**
NAPLES: It is printed on the boxes.
NAPLES: Is also true. Original. Italian. Is me.
OLD: Which means somebody knows where you're from.
NAPLES: Everybody knows where I'm from. I tell them.
OLD: Then we go back to where it began. Contessa. **Naples.**
NAPLES: ...Finalmente. Somebody takes me home.
```

### Scene 5: Battered team -> Long Beach

**LOGIC (provisional, his reading, not proof):** He lays out every ticket: twelve customers, twelve spellings, no two alike. Tonight's ticket spells the name correctly, **K-A-T-E-L-Y-N**, at one house in Long Beach. His chain of reasoning: nobody has spelled it right yet, so she is either the only honest customer in America or the one who wrote everyone else's names. A skeptical aside from Stan gets turned into more suspicion. "You coming?" lets the old slice earn the stale line.

```
comp: wide
OLD: Look at you. Look at what's left of you.
OLD: Good. Scars mean we're close.

comp: board
OLD: Twelve tickets. Twelve spellings. No two alike. Aliases, every one.
OLD: Then tonight's comes in. Long Beach, California. One house. Spelled **correctly**.
OLD: Twelve customers who can't spell it. One who can.
OLD: Either she's the most honest customer in America, or she wrote the others' names.
DEEP: Or she's just a person who can spell.
OLD: That's exactly what she'd want you to think.

comp: wide
OLD: One slice won't do it. This takes all four of you. Every crumb. Find out where it leads.
JUMBO: A point of order. Will you be joining us in the field, sir?
NAPLES: Sì. You, who see so much. You come, yes?

comp: oldtimer
DEEP: You never leave the building.
OLD: I left the building.
DEEP: When?
OLD: ...1987. I've been in this box since before you were dough.
OLD: I'm too stale for this shit.
```
That is 12 tickets across 4 cities × 3, all present.

## 7. Lines I'm proudest of

1. **"Lid's open. Get in the box."** It answers Vinny's "Wait, what?" and is the premise of the whole undercover joke, said straight. It never says what happens to him.
2. **"No contact. That means it's working."** The returns line. It turns the most worrying fact in the game into a success metric, and it repeats every delivery without going stale.
3. **"Popular. Like a password is popular."** It's his conspiracy logic in six words, and it lets Vinny's skeptical aside lose without a debunk.
4. **"Everybody knows where I'm from. I tell them."** This line is all of Contessa. It also gives the "Original Italian" leap a reason to land.
5. **"Either she's the most honest customer in America, or she wrote the others' names."** The Long Beach bridge. It starts from a true detail (one spelling is right), reads it two ways, and both are paranoid. It is never proof. Stan's "or she's just a person who can spell" is the skeptic's line, and it gets turned back into suspicion.
