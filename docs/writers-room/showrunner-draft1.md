# Pizza Runner: consolidated script (showrunner cut)

Speaker keys are `old`, `ny`, `deep`, `jumbo`, `naples`, `alewife`. **[T]** marks a line Tommy specifically liked.

## 1. Voice sheet (final)

**The Old Timer.** He is a clipped drill sergeant with a noir detective's filing habit. He states insane things as settled paperwork ("Intel goes on the board"). Any evidence for the theory counts, and any evidence against it counts double. He ends beats on "Next." or "Think about it." Real warmth comes in single short flashes ("Kid, I know the feeling"). He wants someone out there at all.

**Vinny Two-Fold (ny).** He is New York bravado that leaks. He drops his g's, says "boss" every other line, and says "a" for "of". He announces he isn't scared right before he is. His tics are "I fold for nobody" and "I ain't sayin' X. I'm sayin' Y", where Y is the confession. He wants respect, and he has never been indoors.

**Big Stan (deep).** He is flat, short and aggrieved, and he defends himself before anyone attacks ("It's not a casserole"). He never raises his voice, because that would take effort. The pauses are the jokes, and his tic is "...Fine." He wants to be taken seriously as a pizza.

**La Contessa (naples).** She is imperious, theatrical and certified. She drops articles ("Is bigger now. Why is it bigger?") and counts seconds ("ninety in the fire"). She calls America "America" and treats anything non-Neapolitan as an insult. "Mamma mia" is rationed. She is quietly homesick, and she softens only about home.

**Phil A. Buster (jumbo).** He is a filibuster in slice form: "Mr. Chairman", "let the record show", "a point of order". He yields his time to himself. Every speech is a grand preamble that gets cut off at the first comma. He wants to be heard in full, once. He is also the slice who reads the fine print.

## 2. Old Timer name (Tommy decides)

1. **Sergeant Gus Overbake** (recommended). Left in the oven too long explains the anger. It sounds like a police sergeant, fits a text-box label as "Sgt. Overbake", and keeps the "stale" punchline intact.
2. **Captain Augie Crumb.** The last crumb matches "down to his last slice", and it reads as a cop.
3. **Chief Al Forno.** "From the oven." It echoes the Naples instruction "Il forno sa."

Dropped: Dutch Oven (a crude second reading, and it dilutes the one swear). Day-Old and Dayold (they spend "too stale" early).

## 5. What changed and why

- **"Doesn't quite come together":** every scene now opens from the previous scene's consequence. Vinny's missing slice gives the intel and the tape. The ticket word gives The Order. Stan's chase gives the speech. The names give the aliases and the "top". Phil's lid gives Naples. The spelling gives Long Beach.
- **"Deep undercover" had no premise:** it now rests on two facts. "Under a cover. Lid down." is the pun, and "The best don't come back at all" is the rule. Silence reads as success to him. It pays off in Vinny's return, the delivered line "No contact. That means it's working", and the CITY_LOST tape. Springfield's "tape on twice / bottom of the stack" is cut as too much machinery. The adventure room's "plain cheese" cover is cut because Stan and Contessa aren't plain.
- **The Order still needs to be his named theory:** "Someone is pulling the strings" stays, and he names them from the word on the ticket. This is a departure from Tommy's "I call them The Order" placement, and the name now follows from something ordinary.
- **Liked lines kept:** "Warm, plain, and full of hope"; a bare "Welcome to Pizza HQ."; "Slices are vanishing all over the world."; "get in close, and find out who" (the closing word is Nintendo's, "who"); "I ain't sayin' I'm rattled..."; "limp crusted"; "Boss, it's hell out there."; "I'm too stale for this shit."; and "fraction of my former self".
- **Moved:** Contessa's "Mamma mia" now sits right after "vanishing all over the world", the line it reacts to.
- **Moved:** "I'm too stale for this shit" is now the end of the whole story, on the "1987" setup, not the end of a briefing.
- **Names:** Phil A. Buster is used, and his name joke is "three names, because one would not hold him". Vinny Two-Fold and Big Stan stay, and La Contessa stays. A real Old Timer name is proposed (section 2).
- **Long Beach bridge:** it is now "twelve disguises, one real name, you drop the disguise at home". It is plainly his reading, Stan's skeptic line is turned back into suspicion, and nothing is confirmed.
- **Number dropped:** "eight slices" is gone from the speech. City pies have six slices and the finale pie has eight, so a number would contradict one of them.
- **Central Park:** the customer is now the Pigeon Lady, and no line depends on a bench.
- **Eating rule:** the customer lines no longer say or imply eating. "Mangia", "Sit, eat" and "too thin" are all cut.
- **Brooklyn:** the instruction no longer quotes "limp crust". It would have stepped on Tommy's liked "limp crusted" line before the Old Timer says it.
- **Cut from the rooms' drafts:** the invented "one of each, it's a party" note, and the premise-less lines "Nobody there looks up", "Only a senator needs all three" and "Someone puts my name on a box".
- **Slice markers, background motion, menus, art direction:** these come from Tommy's separate notes and aren't script changes. The script only adds the tape strip labelled DEEP UNDERCOVER on lost pizzas' photos. Art direction and menu ideas are deferred as he asked.
